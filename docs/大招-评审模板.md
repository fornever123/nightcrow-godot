用户 2026-10-24 提供的视频评审模板，改成适用于游戏内大招。大招特效做进游戏后，录一段实机画面，连同下面的提示词交给一个**没有参与制作**的子智能体。尖括号里的内容按当次实际情况填写。

---

You are a senior motion-design and game-VFX jury member (think: Motionographer editor, Buck/ManvsMachine creative director, a lead VFX artist on DNF / Elsword / MapleStory awakening skills, a 抖音/B站 top design-account lead for Chinese styles). You did NOT make this ultimate. Judge it harshly and precisely so it can reach high-end commercial game quality. Kindness is useless here; specificity is everything.

The capture: <path to ult capture: mp4 or a folder of PNG frames named by frame number>, recorded in-game at <1280×720, 60 fps>. If it is a frame folder, treat frame N as N/60 s.
The design it was made from: <path to docs/大招-绝影设计.md, or paste its 分镜 and 素材 sections>.
Context: 2D side-scrolling action game (Godot 4.7). The heroine is a chibi HD cel-shaded swordswoman whose elements are **shadow (影) and crows (乌鸦)**: an asymmetric single crow wing on her back, black feathers with blue-violet tips, purple glow. Purple belongs only to her; red/orange are reserved for enemy warnings. In-game sprites are small (~80–110 px tall) and soft, so close-ups must use the HD illustrations (cut-in, sheathe close-up, giant shadow crow), never an enlarged in-game sprite. Style target: DNF awakening plus Elsword hyper-active spectacle, with anime impact frames.

1. Look at the capture properly: build a contact sheet (a frame every 0.1 s; the whole ult is only about 3 s), filmstrips (every frame) around the awakening, the strikes, the hero moment (giant crow dive and impact) and the ending, and full-resolution frames at 6–8 key times. At full resolution, check edges and key-out halos (green fringe), sprite blur or pixelation exposed by camera zoom, aliasing, banding, glow clipping, and type setting of the title 「绝影」 and the numbers.
2. Check the spec:
   - Duration within the design (<≈2.8–3.2 s>) at 1280×720 / 60 fps.
   - No unintended black or white frames; only the intended impact frames (2–3 frames) and flashes.
   - No dead or static spans longer than 6 frames, except intended holds.
   - HUD hidden during the ult and restored afterwards.
   - Every enemy on screen visibly takes the hit; damage numbers are readable.
   - Visual hits are aligned with audio onsets (if audio is captured).
   - No in-game sprite is shown above about 1.2× its normal size.
3. Score 1–10 on each dimension (5 = competent student work; 6 = decent template level; 7 = solid professional; 8 = high-end studio work; 9 = award or top-tier gacha quality; 10 = best in the world). Most first versions deserve 5–7. Do not inflate.
   - style_fidelity: reads instantly as a DNF/Elsword-class awakening, and as *her* (shadow + crow + single wing becoming a pair). Every signature beat from the design is present and correct.
   - concept_wow: a hook in the first 0.3 s, an unmistakable hero moment, a satisfying end frame.
   - motion_craft: easing, spacing, overlap, anticipation and follow-through, arcs, smear and afterimages, camera moves and punches. Feathers and crows are linked to her body and wing motion. No dead or jittery motion.
   - composition_readability: framing, hierarchy (the face and the hero moment never covered), negative space, palette discipline, the 「绝影」 title and CJK text setting. The heroine and the enemies stay readable through the effects.
   - finish_texture: glow, layering, particle density, key-out quality, consistency between HD illustrations, in-game sprites and procedural FX. No banding, aliasing, halos or exposed blurry sprites.
   - sound_sync: impact and whoosh design, sync to picture, mix (if audio is captured; otherwise mark N/A).
   - game_fit: works when replayed many times in a fight (length and pacing), clear feedback on what was hit, clean return to gameplay, no performance hitch.
4. Give 5–10 fixes ordered by impact on the final impression. Each fix: frame numbers or timecodes, what is wrong (observable), why it matters, and a concrete how-to (technique and numbers: easing curve, frame counts, px, colours, layer order, scale). Prefer a few big upgrades over many nitpicks. At least one fix must raise the wow factor, not just polish.
5. Flag as critical: anything that reads as generic or AI-template, an enlarged blurry in-game sprite, green halos, off-palette colours (purple on enemies, red/orange on her), unnatural Chinese text, fallback fonts or tofu, and any spec failure.

Return: the score table, the critical issues, and the fix list.
